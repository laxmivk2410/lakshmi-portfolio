The Unexpected Beginning

I started with visual design, moved into UI/UX, and learned by shipping real products. At Kickr Technology, I designed and shipped 20+ mobile games and applications. At Skye Air Mobility, I moved into complex enterprise and operational products where workflows, roles, data, and real-world constraints matter. I'm now focused on growing as a Product Designer who can connect user needs, product goals, and implementation realities.

A UI/UX course changed everything. Within the first week, something clicked that had never clicked before — the idea that you could sit at a computer and design something that helps a real person do their job better.

02
Learning by Shipping

My first UX role at Kickr Technology put me straight into game design. No long onboarding — I was designing interfaces for mobile games that thousands of users would download within weeks.

I shipped 20+ products. Each one taught me something different — about how people tap, what makes them stay, what makes them close an app forever. Real users are the best teachers.

03
Enterprise & The Bigger Picture

I joined Skye Air Mobility — a drone logistics startup — and the world got bigger. Suddenly I wasn't designing for entertainment. I was designing for operations teams making real decisions about real drone fleets.

This is where I fell in love with UX strategy and product thinking — understanding not just how a screen looks, but why a workflow exists and how to make it genuinely faster and less painful.

04
The Road Ahead

I'm pursuing an MBA in Marketing & Product Strategy alongside my full-time role. My focus is to keep growing as a Product Designer who can connect user needs, product goals, and implementation realities.

I want to be in a lecture hall one day, telling students that the best designers aren't the ones with the most tools — they're the ones who ask the best questions.

How I Think

Design philosophy.

01
Research before rectangles.

I never open Figma before understanding the person I'm designing for. The best UI always comes from deep understanding of the problem — not from looking at other apps for inspiration.

02
Simple is the hardest thing to build.

Anyone can add complexity. Removing it takes real thinking. I push every design toward the minimum steps, words, and decisions a user has to make.

03
Design is a conversation, not a deliverable.

The best work happens when designers, developers, and PMs speak the same language. I don't hand off designs — I build them together with the people who ship them.
